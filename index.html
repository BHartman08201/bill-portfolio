02 / Approach

One product. One experience.

Hardware and software are often designed by separate teams. Users experience them as one thing. I work across that boundary.

  1. Understand

    Observe the people, environment, constraints, and real work behind the brief.

  2. Structure

    Turn complex requirements into a clear model before polishing the surface.

  3. Make

    Prototype in the medium that answers the question—sketches, models, interfaces, or code.

  4. Refine

    Test the whole experience and carry decisions consistently from detail to system.
